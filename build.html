Every Lead, Then the Visit

Every enquiry lands in one inbox — your website, ads, marketplaces. Book the site visit straight onto the schedule, and one strip shows each stage from new enquiry to won work, with the quotes still waiting on a customer, longest first.

Estimates That Sell

Two to six priced options on one quote — the customer picks one and signs, and the deposit follows. Formulas over square feet, wall height and openings; templates; and a follow-up email when a quote goes unanswered.

📅
The Schedule Holds Together

A Gantt chart with linked tasks: move one and the tasks waiting on it move too. Daily logs, selections and change orders sit on the project.

📊
Know Your Numbers

Time, expenses and vendor bills filed by cost code, so each budget line shows its real cost and profit. A work-in-progress report and receivables aging by customer.

Invoices paid online by card or bank, installments with their own due dates, construction-loan draws, AIA G702/G703 billing and lien waivers per payment.

The phone app opens each project — daily log with photos, punch list, change orders, inspections — and saves with no signal. Clients see what you choose in their portal; subs price bids and acknowledge POs in theirs.

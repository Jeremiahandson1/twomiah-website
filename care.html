  • Open shift offers: decline, deadlines, series and a message recordA caregiver can decline an open shift with a reason or hide it; each offer shows Not sent, Pending, Accepted, Declined, Expired or Closed with response counts and an answer-by time; the agency chooses what caregivers see and how many days ahead; offers go by app, text and email with each person's notification settings honoured and every message kept with how it ended; a whole recurring series can be offered at once

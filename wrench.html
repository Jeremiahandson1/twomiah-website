What's Inside

Your Whole Operation.
One Screen.

Dispatch, booking, invoicing, marketing, and your website — all connected. Change something once and it updates everywhere.

📋
One Board for the Day

Every job by technician on one board: assign, reassign, set status. Working hours and time off show on the board, a warning comes up when a job goes to someone without the skill, and jobs are grouped by the zones you work in.

🌐
Customers Book Themselves

Online booking on your website and from the customer portal, with their details already filled in. Reminder texts and emails go out before the jobs you book.

📍
No More “Where’s the Tech?”

The on-my-way text carries a private link showing where the tech is until they arrive. Crew locations and automatic clock-in at the job site from the phone app.

🔁
Revenue Every Month

Maintenance plans whose included visits go on the schedule for each term, and reports on plans sold by rep, renewals coming due and plans that ended.

💰
Quotes That Close

Good / better / best options on one quote — the customer picks and signs, and the deposit follows. A flat-rate pricebook, and a follow-up email when a quote goes unanswered.

📱
The Tech’s Phone Does the Paperwork

Today’s jobs, directions, on my way / on site / done, photos, and checklists built for each kind of job, signed by the customer on site. Invoices paid by card or bank.

📞
Know Which Ads Work

A tracking number for each place you advertise; when a customer calls, the office sees their open jobs and what they owe. For each lead source: the jobs and money it brought, against what you spent.

💬
Texting Built In

A “Text us” box on your website turns each message into a lead you answer by text, from one inbox. (Texting add-on: $10/mo, texts at cost.)

⭐
Reviews After Every Job

Ask for a Google review automatically after a completed job. See revenue per tech, and how many quotes each rep and tech turns into work.
